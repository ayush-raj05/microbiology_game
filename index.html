How to Play — Microbe Battle

You control a friendly microbe. Collect nutrients to score points and evolve. Avoid antibiotics — they deal damage. Pick up power-ups to gain temporary advantages.

Controls: Arrow keys / WASD to move · Space to start · Click buttons to Pause / Reset · Touch buttons for mobile
Goal: Reach higher mutation stages to unlock buffs. Answer quick quizzes for large bonus points. Reach score limit to win.
Mutations: Proto-cell → Flagella → Thick wall → Efflux pumps → Biofilm → Spore

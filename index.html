Welcome — Microbe ID Game

Learn to recognize microbes visually. The game pauses for questions and upgrades difficulty as your score rises. Play & show this to your teacher.

Features: Start screen • Timer • Progress bar • Pause on question • Encyclopedia • Badges
Tip: On mobile tap the canvas to move, or use the bottom D-pad on small screens.

How to play — (simple)

  1. Press Play Game. A microbe drawing will appear.
  2. Choose one of the three answer buttons; the game will tell you if correct.
  3. When a question appears the main animation pauses automatically — take time to answer.
  4. Score increases and difficulty upgrades from Easy → Medium → Hard.
  5. Use the encyclopedia (button at top) to learn more about microbes anytime.

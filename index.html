How to Play Microbe Battle

- On mobile: tap and drag the canvas to move your microbe.
- Use the on-screen D-pad at the bottom for movement and boosting.
- On desktop: use arrow keys or WASD to control your microbe.
- Collect nutrients (green dots) to grow.
- Avoid antibiotics (red rectangles).
- Your microbe evolves features at point milestones (shown in legend above).
- Antibiotic hits reduce evolved features one-by-one, making survival challenging.
- Answer quiz questions during the game to earn bonus points.
